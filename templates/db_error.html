MongoDB Atlas Firewall Detected (IP Not Whitelisted)

MongoDB Atlas drops connections with TLSV1_ALERT_INTERNAL_ERROR when your current internet IP address is not on the Atlas Network Access list.

Quick Fix in MongoDB Atlas (Takes 30 seconds):
  1. Open cloud.mongodb.com in your browser.
  2. In the left sidebar under Security, click Network Access.
  3. Click the green "Add IP Address" button.
  4. Click "Allow Access from Anywhere" (this enters 0.0.0.0/0 so dynamic mobile/Wi-Fi IPs never get blocked).
  5. Click "Confirm" and wait ~30 seconds for the status to turn Active.
  6. Refresh this page!
