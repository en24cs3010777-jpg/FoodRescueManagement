Two Roles, One Seamless Platform

Whether you have extra food or want to rescue meals for your community, our unified portal makes it effortless.

🍲

Food Providers

Ideal for restaurants, mess halls, wedding venues, caterers, grocery stores, and households with extra edible food.

  • ✔️ Quick surplus listing with quantity & pickup times
  • ✔️ Track real-time claims and pickup statuses
  • ✔️ Reduce food disposal costs and environmental footprint
Register as Provider
🤝

Food Seekers

Designed for NGOs, shelter homes, community volunteers, charity organizers, or anyone in need of edible surplus food.

  • ✔️ Real-time search and filter by location and food type
  • ✔️ Instant 1-click claim with atomic MongoDB safety
  • ✔️ Direct pickup coordination with provider contacts
Register as Seeker

How The System Works

1️⃣

Unified Account

Sign up once and switch between Provider & Seeker modes on your dashboard.

2️⃣

Post or Browse

Providers post details; Seekers browse and filter available surplus food.

3️⃣

Claim & Coordinate

Seeker claims an item; provider and seeker details are shared for pickup.

4️⃣

Collect & Rescue

Food is collected and marked as rescued. Zero food waste accomplished!
